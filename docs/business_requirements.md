# Business Requirements

## 1. Business Problem

An e-commerce marketplace serves customers through a network
of sellers across multiple geographic regions.

Customer experience varies across orders, with concerns including
delivery delays, poor review scores, cancellations, and limited
repeat purchasing.

Management lacks a consolidated analytical view of the operational
factors associated with these outcomes.

The objective of this project is to analyze customer, order, seller,
product, delivery, payment, and review data to identify patterns
associated with poor customer experiences and provide data-driven
recommendations for improving marketplace operations.

---

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyze overall customer experience.
- Evaluate delivery performance.
- Identify patterns associated with poor customer ratings.
- Compare seller performance.
- Analyze customer repeat-purchase behaviour.
- Identify operational areas requiring attention.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

---

## 3. Stakeholders

### Operations Manager
Needs visibility into delivery delays and operational performance.

### Customer Experience Manager
Needs to understand factors associated with poor customer ratings.

### Seller Management Team
Needs to identify sellers with poor operational performance.

### Senior Management
Needs a high-level view of marketplace performance and areas
requiring attention.

---

## 4. Business Questions

### Customer Experience

1. What is the overall distribution of customer review scores?
2. What percentage of orders receive poor ratings?
3. Are late deliveries associated with lower review scores?
4. What characteristics are common among poorly rated orders?

### Delivery Performance

5. What percentage of delivered orders arrive late?
6. What is the average delivery time?
7. Which geographic regions experience the highest delay rates?
8. Which product categories experience longer delivery times?

### Seller Performance

9. Which sellers have the highest late-delivery rates?
10. Which sellers consistently receive poor customer ratings?
11. Which sellers should be prioritized for operational review?

### Customer Behaviour

12. What proportion of customers make repeat purchases?
13. Do customers experiencing late deliveries show different
repeat-purchase behaviour?
14. Do customers giving poor ratings return at different rates?

### Root-Cause Analysis

15. Are poor customer experiences concentrated among particular
sellers, locations, product categories, or delivery conditions?
