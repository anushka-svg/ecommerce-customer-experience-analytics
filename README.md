# E-Commerce Customer Experience & Operations Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce marketplace data to understand
customer experience and operational performance.

The analysis focuses on identifying patterns related to delivery
delays, customer ratings, seller performance, cancellations, and
repeat purchasing.

The goal is to transform raw marketplace data into actionable
business insights and recommendations.

## Business Problem

An e-commerce marketplace is experiencing inconsistent customer
experiences across orders.

Management wants to understand:

- What factors are associated with poor customer ratings?
- Where are delivery delays occurring?
- Which sellers require operational attention?
- How does delivery performance relate to customer experience?
- How do customer experiences relate to repeat purchasing?
